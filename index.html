Pipi

Calm email that shows its work

Pipi is an email app for iPhone and Android that sorts your Gmail into four piles and tells you, in one line, why each email landed where it did. Nothing important gets buried, and nothing leaves your phone.

  • PeopleMail from people you know and have written to.
  • UpdatesReceipts, sign-in codes, deliveries, account notices.
  • NewslettersThings you subscribed to, to read when you have time.
  • New sendersAnyone new waits here once, until you decide where they belong.

How it works

You sign in with Google, and Pipi reads your Gmail directly from Google onto your phone. Sorting, search and learning all happen on the device. Move an email and Pipi remembers that sender; accept one address from a company and Pipi won't ask about the rest. When you archive an email, mark it read or move it to Trash, Pipi does the same in Gmail. It sends only what you write and tap Send on, never deletes anything for good, and its piles never change your Gmail labels.

Your data

Pipi has no server that sees your mail. It stores mail in its private storage on your phone and your sign-in in the phone's Keychain. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Pipi's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
